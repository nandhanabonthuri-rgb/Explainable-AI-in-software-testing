What is Explainable AI?

Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) refers to AI systems whose decisions and predictions can be easily understood by humans. Unlike traditional black-box AI models, XAI provides transparency, clarity, and trust.

Role of XAI in Software Testing

  • Explains why a test case failed
  • Helps identify defect patterns
  • Improves debugging efficiency
  • Supports test case prioritization

Advantages of XAI

✔ Better transparency in testing decisions

✔ Increased trust in AI-based testing tools

✔ Faster fault identification

✔ Improved software quality
